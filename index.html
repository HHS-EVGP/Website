Student-Driven Success

We are the HHS Electric Vehicle Grand Prix team.

What We Do

We are a group of students at Harrisonburg High School who design, build, and race an electric car to compete in the Electric Vehicle Grand Prix. In February of 2024, we participated in the second annual race in the UAE. As of 2026, we are a five-year-old team.

Sharing Our Knowledge

As student innovators, we believe that lessons and experience should be shared, not guarded as secrets. For this reason, we publish our code and designs on GitHub and are working on creating instructional resources for other teams.
